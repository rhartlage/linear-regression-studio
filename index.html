3 · Read the evidence

The current model

Fitted equation

Coefficient t-tests

Two-sided tests of coefficient = 0, conditional on the other included terms. Interactions make main effects conditional on the stated centering values.

ANOVA

The overall F-test compares this fitted model with an intercept-only model. Conventional t/F inference assumes independent errors, a correct mean model, constant error variance, and normal errors for exact small-sample tests.

Model-change history

Model selection is exploratory. Repeated p-value screening can overfit; final p-values and intervals do not account for that selection. Compare adjusted R² and residual diagnostics, and validate on new data. Association is not causation.

4 · See the model

Predictions & sensitivity

The model slice varies the selected predictor while holding all others at the reference settings below. Raw observations may not lie near this slice because their other predictor values differ.

Compare an externally calculated simple-regression line

For a one-predictor model, enter a slope and intercept in original units. The comparison line is visual only; inference always describes the fitted least-squares model.

Tornado values & interpretation

For each original predictor, vary it from its observed minimum to maximum while holding other inputs fixed. Recompute interaction and squared terms, including any interior quadratic turning point. Bars span minimum to maximum predicted y, ranked by width. This is conditional sensitivity, not significance or causal importance. With correlated predictors, these combinations may be unrealistic.

5 · Challenge the assumptions

Residual diagnostics

The normal probability plot checks the shape of residuals, not whether x or y is normally distributed. A roughly straight pattern supports the normal-error assumption; it does not prove it. Curves, changing spread, and unusual observations deserve investigation.

Observation-level results

Leverage measures unusual predictor combinations. Investigate unusual observations in context; do not remove them just to improve a fit.

Biblical/Ethical Stewardship and Integrity

Report association without implying causation, disclose influential points and assumption concerns, and avoid extrapolating beyond the observed range. A useful business recommendation must remain truthful about what the model cannot establish.
